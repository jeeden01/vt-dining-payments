First-Year On-Campus Unlimited Plan Guide

First-Year Unlimited Plan
Unlimited Meal Plan Breakdown
Tap for complete breakdown of plan features and allowances.
Overview: First-year on-campus dining plan providing continuous, unrestricted access to primary campus dining halls alongside weekly retail meal swaps and declining balance spending.

What's Included:
  • Unlimited AYCTE Swipes: Unrestricted daily entry access at D2 and Owens Dining Hall.
  • 2 Weekly Meal Exchanges: Converts 1 swipe into a complete combo meal + drink at select campus retail locations each week.
  • 5 Guest Meals: Semester allowance to host visiting family or friends at AYCTE locations.
  • Declining Balance Account: Built-in funds for a la carte items, coffee, and snacks across campus.
👉 Primary Use: Use your unlimited swipes for primary daily meals at D2 and Owens.
Unlimited Swipe Dining Centers
D2 & Owens Dining Halls
Tap for D2 and Owens swipe access and station details.
Overview: Virginia Tech's core dining centers accepting unlimited entry swipes for daily meals.

Venues Covered:
  • D2 (Upper Dietrick Hall): All-You-Care-To-Eat hall featuring 8 international live-action food stations.
  • Owens Dining Hall: Major campus dining hub featuring multiple specialty counters, entrees, and customized meal options.
👉 How to Pay: Tap your Hokie Passport ID card or Grubhub app barcode at entry.
Retail Meal Exchanges
Perry Place, Turner Place & Retail Venues
Tap to see where and how to use your 2 weekly Meal Exchanges.
Overview: Converts 1 of your meal swipes into a designated combo meal + fountain drink at select retail food counters across campus.

Popular Meal Exchanges:
  • Smoke (Perry Place): Entree (pulled pork/turkey) + 2 sides + drink.
  • Street Taqueria & Origami: Taco platter or Teppanyaki entree + drink.
  • Turner Place & Express Venues: Designated sandwich, specialty bowl, pizza, or burger combos.
👉 Weekly Allowance: 2 Meal Exchanges per week. Balance resets every Sunday!
Declining Balance Dollars
A La Carte Venues & Coffee Shops

Overview: Built-in funds included with the Unlimited plan to buy single items, snacks, coffee, or meals at retail centers.
