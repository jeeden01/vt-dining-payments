Understanding Meal Swaps, Declining Balance & Payment Options

How Meal Exchanges (Meal Swaps) Work

Meal Exchanges allow students on Unlimited dining plans to use a swipe for a combo meal at participating select à la carte venues instead of an all-you-care-to-eat dining center like D2.

Weekly Meal Exchange Allowances:
  • Unlimited Plan: 2 Meal Exchanges per week
  • Unlimited Plus Plan: 3 Meal Exchanges per week
  • Combo Eligibility: Exchanges cover a pre-designated entry, side, and drink at designated locations.
  • Weekly Reset: Unused meal exchanges reset each week and do not carry over.

Payment Methods On Campus

  • Meal Swaps: Cover complete designated combo meals at participating venues.
  • Declining Balance / Flex Dollars: Funds built into primary meal plans that work like a debit card and receive built-in dining discounts.
  • Dining Dollars: Supplemental funds for off-campus students with a 5% discount at all dining centers.
  • Hokie Passport General Funds: Flexible account used for campus printing, laundry, bookstore, and dining (no dining discount applied).
