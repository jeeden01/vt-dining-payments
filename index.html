First-Year On-Campus Unlimited Plans Guide

First-Year On-Campus Plans
Unlimited vs. Unlimited Plus
Tap for side-by-side comparison of freshman dining plan features.
Overview: All first-year students living on campus are required to choose an Unlimited Dining Plan for complete dining hall access.

Plan Features:
  • Unlimited Plan (Default): Unlimited AYCTE swipes + 2 weekly Meal Exchanges + 5 Guest Meals per semester + built-in Declining Balance account.
  • Unlimited Plus Plan: Unlimited AYCTE swipes + 3 weekly Meal Exchanges + 5 Guest Meals per semester + expanded Declining Balance account.
👉 Default Selection: First-year on-campus residents are automatically enrolled in the Unlimited Plan unless Unlimited Plus is selected in Hokie SPA.
AYCTE Dining Center
D2 (Dietrick Hall Upper Level)
Tap for D2 Unlimited All-You-Care-To-Eat swipe access details.
Overview: Virginia Tech's primary All-You-Care-To-Eat venue featuring 8 international live-action food stations.

Access: Both Unlimited Plans grant unrestricted daily entry swipes into D2.
👉 How to Pay: Tap your Hokie Passport ID card or Grubhub app barcode at the entrance.
Retail Meal Exchanges
Perry Place, Turner Place & Retail Venues
Tap to see where and how to use weekly Meal Exchanges.
Overview: Converts 1 of your weekly swipes into a designated combo meal + fountain drink at select retail food counters across campus.

Popular Meal Exchanges:
  • Smoke (Perry Place): Entree (pulled pork/turkey) + 2 sides + drink.
  • Street Taqueria & Origami: Taco platter or Teppanyaki entree + drink.
  • Turner Place & Express Venues: Designated sandwich, specialty bowl, pizza, or burger combos.
👉 Weekly Allowance: 2 exchanges/week (Unlimited) or 3 exchanges/week (Unlimited Plus). Resets weekly!
Declining Balance Dollars
A La Carte Venues & Coffee Shops
Tap to see where to spend your built-in Declining Balance Dollars.
Overview: Built-in funds included with every Unlimited plan to buy single items, snacks, coffee, or meals at retail centers.

Where to Use:
  • West End Market: Chop house, pasta bistro, and carved entrees.
  • Owens Food Court & Hokie Grill: Specialty food stalls and Pizza Hut.
  • Deet's Place & Xpress Lane: Specialty coffee, ice cream, and grab-and-go markets.
👉 How to Pay: Order via Grubhub mobile app or swipe ID at register.

Hokie Passport Account
Laundry, Printing & Campus Stores
Tap for non-dining campus spending (dorm laundry & printing).
Overview: Separate campus debit account used for non-food campus necessities.

Common Uses:
  • Dorm washers and dryers (via Speed Queen App / ID tap)
  • Newman Library printing stations
  • Campus vending machines & University Bookstore
