Hokie Passport Account
Laundry, Printing & Non-Dining
Tap for laundry, printing, and bookstore spending rules.
Details: Separate debit account that rolls over semester-to-semester. Covers all non-dining campus needs.

Where to Use:
  • Dorm washers and dryers (or Speed Queen App)
  • Newman Library printers
  • Campus vending machines & University Bookstore
👉 How to Pay: Swipe Hokie Passport ID card or tap in app.
💡 ESSENTIAL PRO-TIPS
  • Grubhub Setup: Go to Account ➔ Campus Dining ➔ Virginia Tech. Log in and link Hokie Passport + Credit Card.
  • Finding Meal Swaps: In the Grubhub app, look specifically for the section header labeled "Meal Exchange" under participating venues.
  • Track Dining Dollar Balance: West End, Chick-fil-A, & Deet's take Dining Dollars or Credit Cards. Check real-time balance at wallet.vt.edu.
