2026–2027 Unlimited Dining Plans
Unlimited & Unlimited Plus Plans
Required for 1st-Year On-Campus Students. Tap for details.
Overview: First-year residents select an Unlimited Plan, featuring unlimited entry to AYCTE dining halls, weekly meal exchanges, and built-in Declining Balance Dollars.

Plan Options:
  • Unlimited ($3,413/sem): Unlimited AYCTE swipes + $225 Declining Balance + 2 Meal Exchanges/week + 5 Guest Meals.
  • Unlimited Plus ($3,731/sem): Unlimited AYCTE swipes + $600 Declining Balance + 3 Meal Exchanges/week + 5 Guest Meals.
👉 Default Plan: First-year students living on campus are defaulted to the Unlimited Plan.
Unlimited Swipes Center
D2 (Dietrick Hall Upper Level)
Tap for D2 Unlimited All-You-Care-To-Eat swipe rules.
Overview: Flagship All-You-Care-To-Eat (AYCTE) venue with 8 live-action international stations.

Access: Unlimited Plan holders enjoy unlimited daily entry swipes.
👉 How to Pay: Tap Hokie Passport ID card or Grubhub app barcode at the gate for unlimited access.
Meal Exchange Program
Retail Meal Exchanges (Perry Place, Turner, etc.)
Tap to see where to use your 2–3 weekly Meal Exchange swipes.
Overview: Converts 1 meal swipe into a designated multi-item combo + fountain drink at select retail stations.

Popular Exchange Combos:
  • Smoke (Perry Place): Pulled pork/turkey sandwich + 2 sides + drink.
  • Street Taqueria / Origami: Taco platter or Teppanyaki entree + drink.
  • Turner Place & Express Venues: Designated sandwich, soup/salad, or pizza combos.
👉 Limit: 2 or 3 exchanges per week (resets weekly depending on your Unlimited Plan tier).
Declining Balance Plans (Maroon / Orange)
Maroon & Orange Plans (Upper-Year & Off-Campus)
Tap for declining balance options for non-freshmen.
Overview: Dollar-for-dollar declining balance debit plans for upper-year residents and off-campus Hokies.

Options:
  • Maroon ($2,090/sem): $2,090 Declining Balance Dollars (on/off campus upper-year).
  • Maroon Plus ($2,646/sem): $2,646 Declining Balance Dollars.
  • Orange / Orange Plus ($529 / $953): Supplemental plans with 10%–15% bonus dollars.
👉 Usage: Works at ALL campus venues (West End, Owens, Deet's, Hokie Grill, etc.).
